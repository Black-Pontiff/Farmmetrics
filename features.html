What's inside

Four modules. One connected platform.

Farm Metrics isn't four separate tools bolted together — a single sensor reading can trigger an AI recommendation, update your inventory, and write a new block to the ledger, all in the same cycle.

Module 1

Precision Farming

Real-time environmental data from the field, without needing to walk it.

  • Soil moisture, temperature, humidity and light readings update automatically from Arduino-based sensor nodes.
  • Data reaches the dashboard through a Raspberry Pi gateway, so logging continues even with patchy connectivity.
  • Set custom thresholds per field so you're alerted only when a reading actually needs attention.
  • Season-long history charts show how a field behaved over time, not just today.
Sensor typesCapacitive soil moisture, DHT22 temperature/humidity, LDR light sensor
Sample rateEvery 30 seconds by default, configurable per field
GatewayRaspberry Pi 4, buffers locally up to 72 hours offline
Data retention7 days (Free) · 12 months (Pro) · Unlimited (Enterprise)
Module 2

Blockchain Traceability

A record buyers and auditors can check for themselves.

  • Every sensor batch, AI recommendation and inventory change is hashed and appended automatically — no manual entry.
  • Buyers can verify a batch's history without needing access to your internal systems.
  • Tampering with a past record breaks the hash chain immediately, so integrity issues surface on their own.
  • Exportable certificates of provenance for produce sold under a traceability claim.
Hashing algorithmSHA-256
Ledger typePermissioned, append-only
VerificationPublic, read-only lookup per batch
Block intervalNew block every logging cycle, or on demand
Module 3

AI Recommendations

Guidance built from your field's own history, not a generic calendar.

  • Recommendations are generated from your own sensor history and crop stage, not a one-size-fits-all schedule.
  • Each one is labelled by urgency — critical, warning, notice or optimal — so your team knows what to act on first.
  • Models improve as more seasons of local data accumulate on your farm.
  • Delivered in plain language, with the reasoning behind each recommendation one tap away.
Update frequencyEvery 6 hours (Free) · Hourly (Pro) · Real-time (Enterprise)
Model inputsSoil, weather, crop growth stage, historical yield
OutputRanked recommendation list per field
ExplainabilityReasoning summary attached to every recommendation
Module 4

Farm Management

Inventory and finances in the same place as your field data.

  • Track seed, fertiliser, pesticide and water stock levels without a separate spreadsheet.
  • Log expenses and revenue by field or by season to see real margins, not estimates.
  • Compare yield across fields and seasons to spot what's actually working.
  • Built-in categories for Zimbabwe's common crops — maize, soybean, tobacco and horticulture — from day one.
Inventory categoriesSeed, fertiliser, pesticide, water, fuel
Financial reportsRevenue, expenses and net margin by field or season
Data exportCSV export (Pro and Enterprise)
Multi-user accessRole-based permissions (Pro and Enterprise)
