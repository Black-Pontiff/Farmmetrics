Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can move between Free and Pro at any time from your account settings; changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Moving to Enterprise involves a short onboarding call so we can size the plan to your operation correctly.
Is the sensor hardware included?
The Free plan includes one starter sensor node so you can try real data before committing. Pro and Enterprise plans include the sensor kit for each monitored field as part of onboarding; replacement or additional nodes are billed separately at cost.
Do I need internet access at the farm?
You need intermittent connectivity for the Raspberry Pi gateway to sync — it doesn't need to be constant. The gateway buffers up to 72 hours of readings locally and forwards them once a connection is available. See How It Works for more detail.
What payment methods do you accept?
We support major cards and mobile money transfer for Zimbabwean customers. Enterprise plans can be invoiced directly. Get in touch through the Contact page for payment arrangements specific to your organisation.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
Book a demo and we'll set you up with a time-limited Pro trial on one of your fields, so you can see full blockchain traceability and hourly AI recommendations before deciding.

Not sure which plan fits your farm?

Tell us how many fields and what you grow — we'll recommend a starting point.
