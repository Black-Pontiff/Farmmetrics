Observe. Diagnose. Act.

An Arduino-to-cloud pipeline that turns raw field conditions into a specific next step.

  1. 1

    Observe

    Arduino-based sensor nodes in your fields measure soil moisture, temperature, humidity and light every few seconds and pass readings to a nearby gateway.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    A Raspberry Pi gateway filters and forwards the data to the cloud, where the Farm Metrics AI compares it against your crop's growth stage and history.

  3. 3

    Act

    You get a specific recommendation — and every reading, action and inventory change is hashed onto the blockchain automatically.
