About Farm Metrics

Built in Harare, for every farmer who's ever had to guess

Farm Metrics (Private) Limited is a Zimbabwean agritech company. We combine IoT sensors, AI and blockchain into one platform that's affordable for a smallholder and rigorous enough for an exporter.

Why we started

Commercial farms with budgets for consultants, weather stations and lab soil tests already make decisions with real data. Zimbabwe's roughly 500,000 smallholder farmers are mostly left with judgement and habit — not because the data doesn't matter to them, but because the tools have never been priced or designed for them.

We registered Farm Metrics (Private) Limited in Harare to close that gap: low-cost Arduino sensors instead of expensive weather stations, an AI layer that turns raw readings into plain-language advice instead of a dashboard full of charts nobody has time to read, and a blockchain ledger that lets a smallholder prove a claim about their produce as convincingly as a large exporter can.

We started with the crops already grown across most Zimbabwean smallholdings and cooperatives — maize, soybean, tobacco and horticulture — and built the platform to expand field by field, not feature by feature.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To put affordable precision agriculture, verified traceability, and AI-driven insight into the hands of every farmer — starting in Zimbabwe.

Our vision

A future where smallholder and commercial farmers alike make every planting, watering and selling decision backed by real data — not guesswork.
