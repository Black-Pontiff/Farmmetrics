Under the hood

From a sensor in the soil to a screen in your hand

Farm Metrics runs on a three-tier pipeline — edge, fog and cloud — so it keeps working even where connectivity is unreliable.

The data pipeline

Arduino → Raspberry Pi → Cloud API → Dashboard

Edge, fog and cloud — in plain language

Three computing tiers, each doing the part it's best at.

Edge — the Arduino

The sensor node in the field does the smallest, fastest job: reading soil moisture, temperature, humidity and light, and passing the numbers on immediately. Nothing is analysed here — it just measures and sends.

Fog — the Raspberry Pi

A gateway near the field collects readings from every sensor node, filters out noise, and stores them locally if the internet connection drops. When connectivity returns, it forwards a clean batch to the cloud.

Cloud — the heavy lifting

The cloud API is where the AI models run, where recommendations are generated, and where every event is hashed onto the blockchain — work that needs more computing power than a field device can offer.

How the ledger stays honest

Traceability only means something if the record can't be quietly edited.

Hashing

Every sensor batch, recommendation and inventory change is run through a one-way hash function before it's stored, turning the record into a fixed-length fingerprint that changes completely if even one character is altered.

SHA-256 chaining

Each new block includes the hash of the block before it. Altering an old record breaks every hash that follows, which makes tampering detectable rather than invisible.

Role-based access

Field workers, farm managers and buyers see different views of the same ledger. Only authorised roles can trigger actions; everyone with a link can verify a batch's history.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet access on my farm to use Farm Metrics?
No. The Raspberry Pi gateway buffers readings locally for up to 72 hours if your connection drops. Once connectivity returns, everything syncs automatically. You'll see slightly delayed data during an outage rather than missing data.
What happens if my Raspberry Pi loses connectivity for longer than that?
The gateway prioritises the most recent readings once its local storage fills up, and flags the gap in your dashboard's history view so you know exactly which period is missing rather than seeing an inaccurate gap-free chart.
How often do sensors report data?
Every 30 seconds by default. You can adjust the interval per field — shorter intervals give finer detail but use more battery and data; longer intervals suit low-power, low-connectivity sites.
What sensors are compatible with Farm Metrics?
The standard kit ships with a capacitive soil moisture probe, a DHT22 temperature and humidity sensor, and an LDR light sensor, all wired to an Arduino-compatible board. Enterprise customers can integrate additional sensors — including pH probes and third-party weather stations — through our gateway API.
How does the blockchain traceability actually work?
Every logged event — a sensor batch, an AI recommendation, an inventory or financial change — is hashed with SHA-256 and appended to a permissioned, append-only ledger. Each new entry references the hash of the one before it, so the chain reveals any attempt to alter history.
Is my farm data private and secure?
Yes. Your raw operational and financial data is only visible to roles you authorise. What's shareable with buyers or auditors is the traceability record for a specific batch — not your whole farm's internal data. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.
Can I use my own sensors instead of the Farm Metrics kit?
On Enterprise plans, yes — our gateway API accepts readings from compatible third-party hardware. On Free and Pro plans, the platform is tuned specifically for the standard Arduino-based kit to keep setup simple.
How accurate are the AI recommendations?
Recommendations improve as more seasons of data accumulate for your specific fields — early recommendations lean more heavily on general agronomic models, while established fields benefit from your own historical patterns. Every recommendation includes a short explanation of the reasoning behind it, so your team can apply judgement alongside it.
Do I need technical or programming knowledge to set this up?
No. The sensor kit arrives pre-configured, and setup is a guided process of placing sensors and powering on the gateway. Our onboarding team supports Pro and Enterprise customers through initial installation.
What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?
You can export your sensor history, inventory records and financial logs as CSV files at any time before cancelling. Blockchain-logged traceability records remain independently verifiable, since they exist on the ledger rather than solely in your account. See our Terms of Service for details.
